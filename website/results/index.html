What the numbers mean — and what they do not

Submitted FPS

Frames submitted by the application. The counter alone does not prove that every frame was displayed on the monitor.

Translation time

Time for a translation request. The new language test measures text translation only, with OCR, frame generation and NR off. Model loading is listed separately.

Caption delay

May include OCR, queuing, translation, stabilization and reading hold. It can be much longer than model inference alone.

Median and P95

The median is the middle value. P95 describes the slower end: 95% of samples were at or below it. P95 from only 12 lines is exploratory.

Meaning and fluency scores

Language Lab scores meaning and fluency separately: 12.5 × their sum. The memory test uses one 0–4 assessment, multiplied by 25. Each report describes its rubric; do not directly compare scores across different sets.

Image comparisons

We compare matching frames and crops. Failed frames remain visible. Historical captures from different settings are not presented as one matched run.
