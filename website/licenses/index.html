DLSS 5 experiment and bundled offline subtitles

NVIDIA VFG

The five original NVIDIA DLLs are taken from the public nvidia-vfx 0.2.0.0 Windows x64 package. Their hashes are verified against the official wheel and their NVIDIA digital signatures are checked during packaging. The original NVIDIA Software License Agreement, AI Product-Specific Terms, model license and third-party notices are in licenses/nvidia-vfg inside the complete ZIP and runtime add-on.

Full component provenance and distribution notes. The runtime supports Ada/Blackwell on Windows; RTX 5070 Ti and RTX 4050 Laptop were tested. This project is not sponsored or endorsed by NVIDIA.

Website and country detection

Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License; notices accompany the files. Font Awesome is provided by Opicebot. No analytics, tracking pixels, accounts or cookies are added. A browser-local preference stores only an explicit language selection.

On first visit, a server-local IP-country lookup chooses Czech for Czechia/Slovakia and English otherwise. Approximate IPv4/IPv6 ranges come from IPdeny; source URLs, retrieval date and hashes are available. The lookup sends no visitor IP to a third party and does not retain it. Normal hosting access logs are separate. A VPN can change the inferred country. Manual language selection always wins; unavailable or unknown location falls back to English.
