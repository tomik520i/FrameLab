Website and country detection

Space Grotesk and IBM Plex Mono are self-hosted under the SIL Open Font License; notices accompany the files. Font Awesome is provided by Opicebot. The FrameLab source adds no analytics, accounts or tracking cookies. The live hosting layer currently injects its own /provoz/tracker.js script; this is separate from FrameLab. A browser-local preference stores the selected language.

On first visit, a server-local IP-country lookup chooses Czech for Czechia/Slovakia and English otherwise. Approximate IPv4/IPv6 ranges come from IPdeny; source URLs, retrieval date and hashes are available. The lookup sends no visitor IP to a third party and does not retain it. Normal hosting access logs are separate. A VPN can change the inferred country. Manual language selection always wins; unavailable or unknown location falls back to English.
