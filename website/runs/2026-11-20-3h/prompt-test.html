3,768 test responses, zero unreviewed. The original 1,747 recorded translations are reviewed separately. Exact duplicate source/output pairs reuse an explicit assessment; every new output was read and assessed.

The 300 development inputs informed the two candidate prompts. The frozen examples prompt then ran against the baseline on the remaining 1,284 pairs. These are fragments from the same recording, not an independent general-language benchmark. One AI reviewer; no verified human reference. Detailed notes are in Czech.

Decision: keep the production prompt. Examples reduced material errors from 21 to 17 on development, but increased them from 74 to 83 on validation: 11 fixed, 20 introduced. Median 396.0 → 401.8 ms. Added rules also worsened development quality. The app prompt was not changed.
