## Timings and denominators
- Frame statistics: 611,216 new-content submissions, 658,481 logged Present calls. Repeated presents are not additional new images. Output rate = new-content count / full duration. Physical scanout is not measured; 165 Hz is the configured display refresh, NOT 165 unique frames/s. DXGI-confirmed FPS is a software confirmation/lower bound.
- Frame callback→Present delay omits capture-card/HDMI buffering and monitor scanout; it is NOT controller-to-photon latency. It is measured for 611,209 valid new-content frames. The first seven have unavailable latency. Frame-gap count excludes the first unavailable/zero interval (611,215 valid intervals).
- OCR timings: all 32,844 logged completed OCR jobs, including empty results. A blank can be correct when no matching subtitle is present. Confidence is the recognizer's own score, NOT measured accuracy. No frames or reference transcript were recorded: OCR precision, recall, CER and WER cannot be established.
- Translation timing: 1,641 uncached jobs, including IPC, separate from 106 cache hits. Cache lookup time is NOT subtitle latency. Caption delay: all 1,741 actual caption-display events, from the originating app capture timestamp to app subtitle display. This includes queues, recognition, translation and reading hold; not hardware scanout.
- 1,747 translations are not 1,747 independent sentences: partial text, revised/merged text and repeated text are present. `source` is the logged English source; the actual prompt additionally appends an ellipsis marker to incomplete sentences. This is reproduced in the retest. `ocr_source` below refers to the latest fragment; `ocr_source` is the matching latest OCR fragment and can be shorter. 1,584 unique (source, translation) pairs; 300 uniformly sampled for review using seed 202611202136, before grading. The original frozen sample remains in quality-review.json. The expanded quality-full-review.json explicitly reviews all 1,584 unique pairs and maps them to all 1,747 jobs. Exactly 163 duplicate jobs inherit the same exact-pair judgment. Zero jobs remain unreviewed; insufficient-context judgments are a separate result, not skipped rows.
- Quality review: one AI reviewer, no blinding or verified human reference. Labels compare Czech with the logged English and selected surrounding logged lines. Includes HUD contamination, not only dialogue. A “good” label does not certify OCR, speaker gender or fluent literary Czech. See the full rubric, uncertainty and all notes in quality-full-review.json. Additional detailed notes are in Czech. The original sample remains unchanged in quality-review.json. No BLEU/accuracy marketing claim is justified.
- System GPU/CPU/power/VRAM figures include OTHER applications. App CPU/RAM excludes helper processes. These are ~1 Hz samples; time_weighted rates use sample intervals. Stage timings use individual events. The main-page rounded GPU mean is time weighted. P95/max resource figures describe observed samples, not instantaneous peaks.
- Phase boundaries use sampled setting-change events (~1 s uncertainty). Short 3×/4× and multi-pass segments are descriptive, not a matched A/B benchmark. Foreground/fullscreen groups are also uncontrolled and do not establish causality.
