The main findings

Czech needs attention

On the same 12 EN → CS inputs, CUNI scores 95.8/100 and Gemma 77.1/100. Gemma often mishandles Czech inflection and sometimes the meaning. This finding applies to this small set.

Fluent Japanese can still be wrong

CS → JA turned arrows into stems and a blue potion into a blue pen. That is why meaning and fluency are scored separately. Both failures appear verbatim below.

Numbers and simple instructions often survived

The model generally preserved three seconds, the arrow count and 18:45. Character relationships, instructions versus past events, and some idioms proved harder.

How we tested

What actually ran

Windows 11 (build 26200), NVIDIA GeForce RTX 4050 Laptop 6 GB, driver 610.62, CUDA. One model at a time, fully on GPU. No OCR, enhancement, FG or translation cache.

Inputs and scope

12 authored sentences, each in six languages. These are not extracted game scripts. Gemma: ten directions, one pass. CUNI: EN → CS only. References were prepared by Codex and have no independent linguistic review.

Timing and warm-up

Wall-clock time around GpuTranslator.translate includes the local HTTP request and response handling. The constructor warms the model once; each direction then warms up on its first sentence outside the scored run. The first measured sentence can therefore benefit from the repeated prompt.

Startup and memory

Gemma: 11.69 s from constructor start; runtime reports 2.56 GiB on GPU. CUNI: 12.47 s and 3.59 GiB. Startup includes verification, import and initial inference. VRAM is the runtime’s model allocation, not total computer usage.

How scores were assigned

One unblinded pass over all 132 outputs, assessed directly by Codex. Meaning 0–4: 4 preserved, 3 minor shift, 2 important distortion, 1 main meaning lost, 0 absent. Fluency 0–4: 4 natural, 3 slight awkwardness, 2 grammar errors, 1 severely malformed, 0 unreadable. Score = 12.5 × (meaning + fluency).

What this cannot establish

132 outputs are not 132 independent situations: there are only 12 meanings and the references are parallel. OCR quality and in-game delay are not measured here. Japanese scores in particular are exploratory and warrant native-speaker review.

Code used: cd2b0954aaed17e5ffa40b1e9120178367b3cc63. Context 2048, temperature 0, seed 20261119; effective options and exact model SHA-256 hashes are in the metadata. No outcome-driven reprompting or best-of-many selection.
